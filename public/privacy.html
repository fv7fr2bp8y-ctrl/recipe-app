TasteMaster 365 Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 July 2026

New Age Studio Ltd operates TasteMaster 365. This policy explains how the service handles personal data.

Data we process

When you create or use an account, we process your name, email address, a securely hashed password, account identifier, session information, and subscription status. We do not store your payment-card number.

Payments

Subscriptions purchased on the TasteMaster website are processed by Stripe. Stripe receives the information required to complete and manage the payment. The Android application is consumption-only and does not offer purchases.

Purpose and sharing

We use this information to authenticate you, provide access to subscribed content, maintain security, answer support requests, and comply with legal obligations. Data is shared only with service providers needed to operate TasteMaster 365, including Stripe, Vercel, and our database provider, or when required by law. We do not sell personal data.

Storage and retention

Account data is retained while your account is active and for the limited period required for security, accounting, dispute resolution, and legal compliance. Sessions use secure cookies. Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are applied.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, export, or deletion of your account and personal data. See Delete account or contact privacy@tastemaster.eu.

Contact

privacy@tastemaster.eu

Политика за поверителност

TasteMaster 365 обработва име, имейл, защитен хеш на паролата, идентификатор на профила, сесия и статус на абонамента, за да осигури вход и достъп до съдържанието. Плащанията в сайта се обработват от Stripe; не съхраняваме номера на банковата карта. Android приложението не предлага покупки.

Можете да поискате достъп, корекция или изтриване на данните си чрез страницата за изтриване на профил или на privacy@tastemaster.eu.
