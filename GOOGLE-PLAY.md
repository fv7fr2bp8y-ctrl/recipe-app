## Billing and access

The Play build is a consumption-only app. It allows an existing subscriber to
sign in and read content, but it does not show Stripe prices, checkout links,
registration, or billing management. Website billing remains available only in
the normal web version.

For App access, select **Yes** and provide the permanent credentials stored in:

`Google Play package/TasteMaster 365/review-account.txt`

State that no purchase, OTP, or additional verification is required.

## Data safety

Disclose collection of name, email address, user ID/account identifier, secure
authentication information, and subscription status. The data is used for
account management, authentication, security, and access control. Data is
encrypted in transit, is not sold, and users can request deletion.

## Digital Asset Links

`public/.well-known/assetlinks.json`

After the first bundle is uploaded, add the Google Play app-signing SHA-256 to
